3. Where the Knowledge Mesh comes from

Two foundations: the architecture of the Data Mesh and the diagnosis of thirty years of knowledge management research.

Type SectionOwner B. RousseauxStatus activeVersion 2.5Updated 2026-11-17
data-meshkm-research

Structure

  • The Data Mesh started from the limits of centralisation
  • Knowledge management research had reached the same diagnosis earlier

Key ideas

The stance.

Accept fragmentation and organise it, rather than pursue the single base. Knowledge is fragmented because it is distributed, situated and alive. Centralising it fights its nature; organising the fragments works with it.

Concepts

  • Data Mesh: Zhamak Dehghani's answer to the limits of centralised data platforms: four principles, presented as technology-agnostic, held together by shared standards and a common platform.
  • Distributed knowledge: Knowledge management research reached the Data Mesh diagnosis earlier: knowledge is distributed, created close to practice and kept alive by communities, which is why technology alone did not deliver.
  • Codification vs personalisation: Hansen, Nohria and Tierney distinguished storing knowledge in documents from connecting the people who hold it. AI loosens that trade-off: the expert stays in charge, the machine does the clerical work.
  • Organised fragmentation: The playbook's stance: accept fragmentation and organise it, rather than pursue the single base. Knowledge is fragmented because it is distributed, situated and alive.
