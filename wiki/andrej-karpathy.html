Andrej Karpathy

Described the "LLM wiki" pattern, the compiled layer behind the pages of a knowledge base, and the pattern this wiki follows.

Type EntityOwner B. RousseauxStatus activeVersion 2.5Updated 2026-11-17
agents

Karpathy's "LLM wiki" is the compiled layer behind the pages of a Knowledge base: knowledge assembled once and kept up to date, rather than reassembled at every question (Compiled knowledge (LLM wiki)). This wiki applies the same pattern to the playbook itself: a raw source kept intact, compiled pages, a schema, an index and a log.
