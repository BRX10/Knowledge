Figure 4: From raw sources to use cases

Layers from bottom to top: traced raw sources, converted to markdown, compiled into domain wikis with an owner each, served through a common MCP access layer to people and agents.

Type FigureOwner B. RousseauxStatus activeVersion 2.5Updated 2026-11-17
figurefederation

What the figure shows. Five layers, read from bottom to top.

  • Raw sources, traced: documents, a conversation, an email.
  • Converted to markdown: .md files.
  • Knowledge bases, a wiki, an owner: two example bases, "HR" and "Legal", each with its owner icon and a wiki of pages, plus a dashed box for "other bases". Dotted lines trace individual pages back to the raw source they come from.
  • Access: a common band labelled "MCP · access by use cases".
  • People and agents: a group of people and a cluster of agents, served from the access layer.

What it means. Each base is owned by a domain and compiles its own traced sources into a wiki; bases coexist under a common access layer rather than being merged. See Knowledge Mesh, Compiled knowledge (LLM wiki) and Model Context Protocol.
