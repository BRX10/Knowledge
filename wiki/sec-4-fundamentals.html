4. Fundamentals we build on

Four established ideas become directly operational once agents are the readers: data, information and knowledge; tacit and explicit; the creation cycle; the lifecycle.

Type SectionOwner B. RousseauxStatus activeVersion 2.5Updated 2026-11-17
foundationskm-researchlifecycle

Structure

  • Data, information, knowledge
  • Tacit and explicit
  • The knowledge creation cycle
  • The lifecycle

Key ideas

Key idea.

Data tells an agent what the company records. Knowledge tells it what the company means.

Concepts

  • Data, information, knowledge: Data records facts, information gives them meaning in a given context, and knowledge is what lets someone act on them. Knowledge carries judgement, and judgement always belongs to someone.
  • Tacit and explicit knowledge: Part of what experts know will never be fully written down. A Knowledge Mesh aims to make explicit what can be, and to show clearly where the rest lives: with whom, in which community.
  • Knowledge creation cycle (SECI): Knowledge is created as it moves between tacit and explicit forms: socialisation, externalisation, combination, internalisation. AI excels at combination and helps externalisation; socialisation remains a human affair.
  • Knowledge lifecycle: Knowledge is born, lives and dies. Six stages mark that life, capture, consolidate, validate, serve, review and retire, each with someone responsible; retirement is the one most often forgotten.
