9. Field guide

Start small and real, treat the mesh as a trajectory, keep knowledge portable, and delegate to agents in three levels.

Type SectionOwner B. RousseauxStatus activeVersion 2.5Updated 2026-11-17
practiceagents

Structure

  • Start small, and real
  • The mesh is a trajectory
  • Interoperability
  • Three levels of delegation

Key ideas

Rule of thumb.

Centralise the means, decentralise the knowledge.

Concepts

  • Start small, and real: The first steps aim at a first base that is useful quickly, owned by someone and measured from the start: one domain, one owner, a one-page charter, the legacy compiled, an evaluation set.
  • The mesh is a trajectory: No company starts with a mesh: it starts with a common foundation, and the mesh appears as domains take ownership one after another. Centralise the means, decentralise the knowledge.
  • Interoperability and portability: A knowledge base is built to last longer than the technologies that serve it. The test: if the tool that built a base disappeared tomorrow, could the knowledge be read, moved and served by another one?
  • Three levels of delegation: Delegating to agents is a gradual decision, taken domain by domain, as trust grows. Assistance, delegation and autopilot are each reached on evidence.

Sources & inspirations

  • Dehghani (2020), Data Mesh principles: the self-serve platform as the condition for domain autonomy, basis of the trajectory; success measured by use across the mesh rather than volume governed
  • Dehghani (2019), monolithic data lake to data mesh: interoperability governed by global standards as a foundational pillar of distributed systems
