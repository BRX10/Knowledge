# Knowledge creation cycle (SECI)

[[ikujiro-nonaka|Nonaka]] and Takeuchi showed that knowledge is created as it moves between its tacit and explicit forms:

- **Socialisation**: people share know-how by working together.
- **Externalisation**: they put it into words.
- **Combination**: explicit pieces are assembled into larger wholes.
- **Internalisation**: people make explicit knowledge their own by using it.

Read with agents in mind, the cycle is uneven. AI excels at combination and is a strong ally for externalisation: an interview, a meeting or a recorded walkthrough can become a draft page. Socialisation remains a human affair: people still learn from people.

> A knowledge strategy that overlooks this produces clean pages and empty expertise.
