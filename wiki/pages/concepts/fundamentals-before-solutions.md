# Fundamentals before solutions

Enterprise search, verified knowledge bases, content platforms with built-in agents and semantic layers already address many of the [[mesh-challenges]]; many are useful, some very good. The playbook deliberately starts one step earlier, with the fundamentals that let an organisation choose, combine and replace them.

> In an agentic and hybrid world, where people and agents work from the same knowledge, one question comes first: what must be true of knowledge for both to rely on it? The answer shapes the choice of tools, and outlasts it.
