# Interoperability and portability

Models, agent frameworks and access protocols will change several times over the life of a base, and knowledge must be able to follow without starting over. That calls for simple, open and evolvable formats: readable text pages, explicit metadata, stable identifiers, links that make sense to a person as well as to a machine, and access through open protocols rather than proprietary connectors.

> If the answer is no, the company does not own its knowledge; its tool does.

Interoperability governed by global standards is a foundational pillar of distributed systems in Dehghani's description of the data mesh. See [[federated-knowledge-governance]].
