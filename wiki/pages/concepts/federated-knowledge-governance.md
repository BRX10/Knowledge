# Federated knowledge governance

The fourth principle transposes federated computational governance. For knowledge, the goal is not to merge bases but to make them composable: shared identifiers, metadata and open access protocols.

It answers the canonical model fantasy among the [[failure-modes]], where a single taxonomy is imposed on every domain. It relies on a governance that embraces several interpretive contexts rather than one canonical model. In practice, bases coexist rather than merge ([[coexisting-bases]]) and stay portable across tools ([[portability]]). Part of the [[five-principles]].
