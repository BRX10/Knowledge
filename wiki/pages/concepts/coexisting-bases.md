# Bases that coexist

Coexistence opens several uses at once:

- some agents consume a base for what it contains, to answer a question or carry out a task;
- others analyse a base on its own, to measure its coverage, its freshness or its gaps;
- others read several bases in a combined context of use, where overlaps and contradictions between domains become visible and can be sent back to the owners concerned.

Merging the bases would lose that view; letting them coexist keeps it. See [[federated-knowledge-governance]] and [[organised-fragmentation]].
