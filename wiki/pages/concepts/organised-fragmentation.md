# Organised fragmentation

> Accept fragmentation and organise it, rather than pursue the single base. Knowledge is fragmented because it is distributed, situated and alive. Centralising it fights its nature; organising the fragments works with it.

The stance follows from [[distributed-knowledge]] and from the [[data-mesh]], which is an intentionally designed distribution rather than a set of silos. Work starts on small perimeters; fragments are brought together at the moment of use (see [[dynamic-context]]) and enriched in the meantime. Bases coexist rather than merge ([[coexisting-bases]]), and the playbook values owned bases more than a single repository ([[mesh-values]]).
