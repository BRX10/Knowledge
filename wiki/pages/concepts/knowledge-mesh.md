# Knowledge Mesh

> A Knowledge Mesh is a decentralised network of living knowledge bases, owned by business domains, each described by a charter and built through auditable contributions from people and agents, federated by common standards so that everyone, human or agent, shares the same governed context.

It stands on two foundations: the architecture of the [[data-mesh]] and the diagnosis of [[distributed-knowledge|knowledge management research]]. It rests on [[five-principles]] and must address a set of [[mesh-challenges]].

Two vocabulary choices avoid confusion. The **Knowledge Mesh** is the whole: the network, the platform and the common rules. The **[[knowledge-base]]** is the unit: what a domain builds, owns and serves. One does not exist without the other.

No company starts with a mesh: it is a [[mesh-trajectory]]. See [[fig-4-multi-base]].
