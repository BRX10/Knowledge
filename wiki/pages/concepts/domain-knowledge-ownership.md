# Domain knowledge ownership

The first principle transposes the Data Mesh's domain-oriented ownership. What changes for knowledge: it is embodied and partly tacit ([[tacit-and-explicit]]), so the owner answers for it, not just stores it.

It answers the central bottleneck among the [[failure-modes]]: a central team carrying the work alone while the people who hold the knowledge are out of the loop. In practice, ownership is held by a [[knowledge-owner]] per base. Part of the [[five-principles]].
