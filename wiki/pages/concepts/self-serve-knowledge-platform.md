# Self-serve knowledge platform

The third principle transposes the self-serve data platform. For knowledge, AI is not only hosted on the platform; it works inside it, drafting pages, linking sources and flagging contradictions.

Dehghani treats the self-serve platform as a precondition of the mesh. Much of it can be assembled from existing tools; what has to be built is the way they are composed, and the standards that let bases move between them (see [[mesh-trajectory]] and [[portability]]). Part of the [[five-principles]].
