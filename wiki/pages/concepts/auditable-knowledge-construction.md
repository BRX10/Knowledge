# Auditable knowledge construction

The fifth principle extends the way the Data Mesh treats quality, which it entrusts to the domains, with explicit quality metrics, rather than to the central certification of "golden" datasets.

Where data quality is measured by metrics and service levels, knowledge quality is read through traceability: who proposed each change, who validated it, from which sources, and how to revert it. Among the management challenges, auditable construction asks how every contribution is proposed, reviewed, merged and reversed ([[mesh-challenges]]). See also [[traceability]]. Part of the [[five-principles]].
