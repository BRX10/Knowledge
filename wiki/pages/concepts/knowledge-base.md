# Knowledge base

A knowledge base has seven parts:

- **A domain and a purpose**: what it covers, for whom, and what it deliberately does not cover.
- **A charter**: purpose, owner, audience, how it is built. It commits the owner, guides the agent that drafts the pages and tells other agents when to consult the base.
- **A Knowledge Owner**: the person who answers for it ([[knowledge-owner]]).
- **Sources**: the documents, sites and contributions it is built from, each inventoried.
- **Pages**: the consolidated knowledge, in an open, readable format, each page linked to its sources ([[compiled-knowledge]]).
- **A contract**: review frequency, how fast a source change reaches the pages, the minimum quality guaranteed. Its health is measured against that contract.
- **States**: active, under review, quarantined, retired. A page or a whole base can be withdrawn from use without stopping everything else.

A base is both an entry point and a destination ([[document-as-output]]), and bases live together by coexistence rather than fusion ([[coexisting-bases]]). See [[fig-5-seven-parts]].
