# AI agent

> AI agents are systems built on AI models that pursue a goal, use tools and act on someone's behalf; for short, the rest of this playbook calls them agents.

What is new for knowledge management is this reader: agents now consume a company's knowledge directly, and act on it at scale. What they do depends on the context they are given, and that context has to be steered and mastered, not left to chance ([[dynamic-context]]).

Around the fundamentals of knowledge management, agents change three things: the reader, the scale and the economics ([[what-agents-change]]). As AI moves from answering to acting, delegating to an agent at scale means accepting that it will act on people's behalf, across many tasks, without every step being checked, while accountability remains human; delegation is therefore gradual ([[delegation-levels]]).
