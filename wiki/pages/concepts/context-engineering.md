# Context engineering

Getting the best out of a language model was first a matter of prompt engineering: a role, a mission, a few paragraphs of background written by hand into a prompt. Practitioners have since moved on to context engineering, the discipline of choosing what goes into a model's working memory. The shift is right about one thing: the quality of an answer depends on the context more than on the model.

> The Knowledge Mesh adds the organisational side that context engineering calls for: where good context comes from, and who stands behind it.

That organisational side is [[dynamic-context]]. The notion is drawn from [[anthropic|Anthropic]]'s writing on context engineering.
