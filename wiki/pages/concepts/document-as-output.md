# The document as output

**An entry point.** Every organisation sits on years of legacy documents. Building a base turns that sediment into an asset, and the exercise reveals the gaps, duplicates and contradictions no one could see. Preparing knowledge for an agent is, almost by accident, the first real audit of what the organisation knows.

**A destination.** Today, the base is fed by documents. Tomorrow, documents will increasingly be produced from it: an onboarding guide, a policy summary, a client brief, generated on demand as a view of what the base holds. One safeguard follows: what is generated never flows back into the base without going through the management plane ([[principle-zero]]); otherwise the base ends up learning from itself.

> The document is no longer the source of knowledge; it becomes one of its outputs.

Between entry and destination, a single base becomes an enabler for many use cases. That is where the return on investment lies: not in one more chatbot, but in an asset that every use draws on.
