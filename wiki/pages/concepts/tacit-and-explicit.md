# Tacit and explicit knowledge

"We can know more than we can tell," wrote [[michael-polanyi|Michael Polanyi]]. Part of what experts know will never be fully written down.

> A Knowledge Mesh aims to make explicit what can be, and to show clearly where the rest lives: with whom, in which community.

This matters more with agents, because the main new consumer is a machine that knows only what has been made explicit ([[what-agents-change]]). Reaching the tacit, finding and connecting the people who hold what is not written, is one of the consumption challenges of the mesh ([[mesh-challenges]]). See [[fig-2-tacit-and-explicit]].
