# The mesh is a trajectory

A mesh usually starts with a common foundation: one platform, a few standards, the first bases, often run by a small central team. That is consistent with the mesh: Dehghani herself treats the self-serve platform as a precondition of it ([[self-serve-knowledge-platform]]). Much of that platform can be assembled from existing tools; what has to be built is the way they are composed, and the standards that let bases move between them.

The mesh appears as domains take ownership one after another, and as the central team moves from building bases to providing the platform and the rules. Starting centrally is a sound first step; the aim is to move beyond it.

> Centralise the means, decentralise the knowledge.
