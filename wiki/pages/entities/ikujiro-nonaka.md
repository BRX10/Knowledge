# Ikujiro Nonaka

Nonaka showed that knowledge is created close to practice, in the interplay between people, not in a central repository ([[distributed-knowledge]]). With Takeuchi, he described knowledge creation as a conversion between tacit and explicit forms: socialisation, externalisation, combination and internalisation ([[seci-cycle]]). The playbook rereads that cycle with agents in mind.
