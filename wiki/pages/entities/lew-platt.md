# Lew Platt

> "If only HP knew what HP knows, we would be three times more productive."

The sentence named a gap every large organisation recognises: a company knows far more than it manages to use. The playbook notes that the quotation is attributed, reported in several variants, with no primary source located. Platt was talking about people, who could still ask a colleague; agents cannot ([[knowledge-as-a-pillar]]).
