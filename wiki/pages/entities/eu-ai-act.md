# EU AI Act

Articles 12, 14 and 26 require high-risk AI systems to log events automatically, to be designed for effective human oversight, and their deployers to keep the logs. Most knowledge agents will not fall into the high-risk category; the playbook still finds it reasonable to aim for the same capability everywhere ([[traceability]]).
