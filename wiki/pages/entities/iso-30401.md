# ISO 30401

The standard sets requirements for establishing, maintaining and improving a knowledge management system, treating it as a managed system with leadership, roles and continual improvement. The playbook cites it as evidence that the discipline is mature ([[knowledge-as-a-pillar]]): the methods exist.
