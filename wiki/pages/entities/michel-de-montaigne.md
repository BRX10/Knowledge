# Michel de Montaigne

> « Plutôt la tête bien faite que bien pleine. »

For Montaigne, a well-made mind is the one that can judge, sort and order what it knows, and put it to use. He does not dismiss knowledge; he places discernment above accumulation. The playbook applies this to companies, which have spent decades filling their heads with drives, intranets and document libraries, and which agents now ask for a well-made one ([[mesh-values]], [[open-questions]]).
