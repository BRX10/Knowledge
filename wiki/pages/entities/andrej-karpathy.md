# Andrej Karpathy

Karpathy's "LLM wiki" is the compiled layer behind the pages of a [[knowledge-base]]: knowledge assembled once and kept up to date, rather than reassembled at every question ([[compiled-knowledge]]). This wiki applies the same pattern to the playbook itself: a raw source kept intact, compiled pages, a schema, an index and a log.
