# Zhamak Dehghani

In 2019, Zhamak Dehghani described the limits of central data platforms; in 2020 she formalised the four principles of the [[data-mesh]]. The playbook draws on her work more than on any other source:

- the failure modes of centralised platforms, named one by one ([[failure-modes]]);
- operational and analytical data as two planes, a precedent for [[principle-zero]];
- the four principles, transposed one by one ([[five-principles]]), and quality assured by the domains, basis of [[auditable-knowledge-construction]];
- the data product owner, responsible for the lifecycle of the domain's data ([[knowledge-owner]]);
- discoverable, self-describing data products with service level objectives, behind the charter and contract of a [[knowledge-base]];
- the self-serve platform as a precondition, basis of the [[mesh-trajectory]], and interoperability governed by global standards ([[portability]]).
