# Figure 6: Agents and the Knowledge Owner

**What the figure shows.** A flow from left to right.

- Inputs: documents, interviews, decisions.
- An agent ("agents draft") turns them into drafts, framed by a **Charter** box (purpose, audience).
- A decision diamond asks "clear-cut?".
- **Yes**: the change goes straight into the **Wiki**.
- **Doubt**: the change goes to the **Knowledge Owner**, drawn as a person holding a validated document, labelled "arbitrates, commits", who then sends it to the Wiki.

**What it means.** The owner does not reread everything: clear-cut changes go through, and the owner handles the exceptions, which keeps the role sustainable. See [[knowledge-owner]].
