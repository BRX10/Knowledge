# Figure 5: The seven parts of a knowledge base

**What the figure shows.** A book in the centre, labelled "knowledge base", with seven labels around it:

- **Knowledge Owner** (top, highlighted): answers for it.
- **Sources**: inventoried.
- **Pages**: linked to sources.
- **Charter**: purpose, audience.
- **Contract**: review, freshness.
- **Domain**: what it covers.
- **States**: active … retired.

**What it means.** A base is more than a set of pages: it has a scope, a charter, an owner, inventoried sources, a contract it is measured against, and states that let any part be withdrawn. See [[knowledge-base]].
