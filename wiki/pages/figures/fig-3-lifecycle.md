# Figure 3: The lifecycle

**What the figure shows.** A vertical sequence of six numbered stages, each with its actors and a short line:

1. **Capture** (agents): agents compile the legacy; people bring interviews.
2. **Consolidate** (agents): deduplicate, link, structure.
3. **Validate** (agents, owner): agents check, the owner commits.
4. **Serve**: to people and agents, according to rights.
5. **Review** (agents, owner): agents assess freshness, the owner refreshes.
6. **Retire** (agents, owner, highlighted): agents flag, the owner decides, and says so.

A dashed arrow loops from Review back to Capture.

**What it means.** The work is shared: agents carry the volume, the owner commits and decides. Retirement is highlighted because it is the stage most often forgotten. See [[knowledge-lifecycle]].
