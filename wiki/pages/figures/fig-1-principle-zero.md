# Figure 1: Principle zero

**What the figure shows.** Two boxes side by side.

- **Managing** (left, in the accent colour): a book standing for the knowledge base, a person and an agent. Below: "build · validate · keep alive · retire", and "deliberate, dated". A small legend marks the book as "markdown format".
- **Consuming** (right): a person and an agent, each with a question mark. Below: "answer · assist · act", "people and agents", "instant".
- An arrow labelled "MCP / API" goes from managing to consuming.
- A dashed arrow returns from consuming to managing, carrying two tags: "gaps" and "errors".

**What it means.** One effort in the management plane serves every use in the consumption plane, and the base learns from its use through feedback rather than from its own answers. See [[principle-zero]].
