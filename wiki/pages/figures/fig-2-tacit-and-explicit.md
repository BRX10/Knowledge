# Figure 2: What can be written, and where the rest lives

**What the figure shows.** An iceberg cut by the waterline.

- Above the water, labelled "written, documented": two peaks holding documents. A bracket marks them as "what the agent sees". Some of the documents look faded.
- Below the water, labelled "tacit, held by people": a much larger mass containing three people, a speech bubble, a light bulb, a clock and a few documents.
- A bracket spanning the whole iceberg reads "what the company knows".

**What it means.** An agent only sees what has been made explicit; most of what the company knows is held by people. A Knowledge Mesh makes explicit what can be, and shows where the rest lives. See [[tacit-and-explicit]].
