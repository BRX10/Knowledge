# 7. The Knowledge Owner

## Structure

- A commitment, dated and open to challenge

## Key ideas

**Key idea.**

> The owner turns a probability into a commitment. That is what an organisation can build on, and what an agent can be held to.

## Concepts

- [[knowledge-owner]]: The person accountable for a base, and the central idea of the playbook: knowledge needs a face. Agents take on most of the stewardship; the owner keeps what cannot be delegated.
- [[dated-commitment]]: What the owner commits to is narrower than truth: "this is what the company holds to be true, as of this date, and I answer for it." Dated, sourced, open to challenge. No owner, no authority.
