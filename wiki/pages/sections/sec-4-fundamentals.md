# 4. Fundamentals we build on

## Structure

- Data, information, knowledge
- Tacit and explicit
- The knowledge creation cycle
- The lifecycle

## Key ideas

**Key idea.**

> Data tells an agent what the company records. Knowledge tells it what the company means.

## Concepts

- [[data-information-knowledge]]: Data records facts, information gives them meaning in a given context, and knowledge is what lets someone act on them. Knowledge carries judgement, and judgement always belongs to someone.
- [[tacit-and-explicit]]: Part of what experts know will never be fully written down. A Knowledge Mesh aims to make explicit what can be, and to show clearly where the rest lives: with whom, in which community.
- [[seci-cycle]]: Knowledge is created as it moves between tacit and explicit forms: socialisation, externalisation, combination, internalisation. AI excels at combination and helps externalisation; socialisation remains a human affair.
- [[knowledge-lifecycle]]: Knowledge is born, lives and dies. Six stages mark that life, capture, consolidate, validate, serve, review and retire, each with someone responsible; retirement is the one most often forgotten.
