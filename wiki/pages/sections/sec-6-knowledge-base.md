# 6. The knowledge base

## Structure

- An entry point
- A destination
- Bases that coexist

## Key ideas

**Key idea.**

> The document is no longer the source of knowledge; it becomes one of its outputs.

## Concepts

- [[knowledge-base]]: The unit and asset of the mesh: small enough to have one owner and one purpose, complete enough to be useful on its own. It has seven parts.
- [[compiled-knowledge]]: The pages of a base are the compiled layer Andrej Karpathy described as an "LLM wiki": knowledge assembled once and kept up to date, rather than reassembled at every question.
- [[document-as-output]]: Building a base turns legacy documents into an asset and audits what the organisation knows. Tomorrow, documents are produced from the base; what is generated never flows back without going through the management plane.
- [[coexisting-bases]]: As bases multiply, the answer is coexistence rather than fusion. Each base keeps its owner, charter and pace; bases share identifiers, metadata and a common way to be reached.
