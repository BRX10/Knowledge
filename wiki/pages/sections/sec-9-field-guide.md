# 9. Field guide

## Structure

- Start small, and real
- The mesh is a trajectory
- Interoperability
- Three levels of delegation

## Key ideas

**Rule of thumb.**

> Centralise the means, decentralise the knowledge.

## Concepts

- [[start-small]]: The first steps aim at a first base that is useful quickly, owned by someone and measured from the start: one domain, one owner, a one-page charter, the legacy compiled, an evaluation set.
- [[mesh-trajectory]]: No company starts with a mesh: it starts with a common foundation, and the mesh appears as domains take ownership one after another. Centralise the means, decentralise the knowledge.
- [[portability]]: A knowledge base is built to last longer than the technologies that serve it. The test: if the tool that built a base disappeared tomorrow, could the knowledge be read, moved and served by another one?
- [[delegation-levels]]: Delegating to agents is a gradual decision, taken domain by domain, as trust grows. Assistance, delegation and autopilot are each reached on evidence.

## Sources & inspirations

- [[src-02-dehghani-2020]]: the self-serve platform as the condition for domain autonomy, basis of the trajectory; success measured by use across the mesh rather than volume governed
- [[src-01-dehghani-2019]]: interoperability governed by global standards as a foundational pillar of distributed systems
