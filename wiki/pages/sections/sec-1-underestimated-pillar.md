# 1. The underestimated pillar

## Structure

- A mature discipline
- Why it was underestimated in the move to AI
- Eight failure modes
- What genuinely changes with AI agents

## Key ideas

**Key idea.**

> Agents raise the bar upstream. The effort moves from consuming knowledge to building it: owned, validated, dated and traceable before any agent reads it.

## Concepts

- [[knowledge-as-a-pillar]]: To understand a situation, an agent needs data, what the company records, and knowledge, what it means and how to act on it. Knowledge deserves the same status as data.
- [[underestimation-reasons]]: Four reasons, offered as a practitioner's reading rather than a measured finding: the model seemed to know, retrieval was treated as engineering, governance followed data, and knowledge management was out of the room.
- [[failure-modes]]: The same patterns recur when AI meets the real corpus. Each has a recognisable symptom, a specific cost for agents, and a principle of the playbook that answers it.
- [[what-agents-change]]: The fundamentals of knowledge management stay. Agents change three things around them, the reader, the scale and the economics, and the effort moves to validation.
