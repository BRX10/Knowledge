# 5. The Knowledge Mesh in brief

## Structure

- The challenges a Knowledge Mesh must address
- Fundamentals before solutions

## Key ideas

**Definition.**

> A Knowledge Mesh is a decentralised network of living knowledge bases, owned by business domains, each described by a charter and built through auditable contributions from people and agents, federated by common standards so that everyone, human or agent, shares the same governed context.

## Concepts

- [[knowledge-mesh]]: A decentralised network of living knowledge bases, owned by business domains, each described by a charter and built through auditable contributions from people and agents, federated by common standards.
- [[five-principles]]: The first four principles transpose those of the Data Mesh. The fifth extends the way the Data Mesh entrusts quality to the domains, rather than to central certification of golden datasets.
- [[domain-knowledge-ownership]]: Principle 1, transposing domain-oriented ownership. Knowledge is embodied and partly tacit: the owner answers for it, not just stores it.
- [[knowledge-as-a-product]]: Principle 2, transposing data as a product. There are two kinds of consumers, people and agents, and no base without a charter.
- [[self-serve-knowledge-platform]]: Principle 3, transposing the self-serve data platform. AI is not only hosted on the platform, it works inside it: it drafts, links and flags contradictions.
- [[federated-knowledge-governance]]: Principle 4, transposing federated computational governance. The goal is not to merge bases but to make them composable, through shared identifiers, metadata and open access protocols.
- [[auditable-knowledge-construction]]: Principle 5, extending quality assured by the domain. Knowledge quality is read through traceability: who proposed each change, who validated it, from which sources, and how to revert it.
- [[mesh-challenges]]: The principles say what a Knowledge Mesh is; the challenges say what it must make possible. Fourteen questions fall into three families: managing knowledge, consuming it, and federating the bases.
- [[fundamentals-before-solutions]]: Solutions already address many challenges. The playbook starts one step earlier, with what must be true of knowledge for people and agents to rely on it; the answer shapes the choice of tools, and outlasts it.
