# MIT NANDA (2025), The GenAI Divide

**Citation.** MIT Media Lab, Project NANDA, The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025 (preliminary report, July 2025).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Reports that most organisations studied see no measurable return from their generative AI initiatives despite significant investment. It attributes the gap largely to systems that do not retain context, adapt or learn from feedback.

**Family.** AI practice and field evidence: compiled knowledge, context engineering, open protocols, and the gap at scale.
