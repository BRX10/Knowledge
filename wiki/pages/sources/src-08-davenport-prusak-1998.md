# Davenport & Prusak (1998), Working Knowledge

**Citation.** Davenport, T. and Prusak, L., Working Knowledge: How Organizations Manage What They Know, Harvard Business School Press (1998).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Offers a practical definition of knowledge as framed experience, values, contextual information and expert insight, distinct from data and information. Describes how organisations generate, codify and transfer knowledge, and why culture matters as much as technology.

**Family.** Information science: what distinguishes knowledge from data and information.
