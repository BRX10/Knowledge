# Dehghani (2020), Data Mesh principles

**Citation.** Dehghani, Z., Data Mesh Principles and Logical Architecture, martinfowler.com (3 December 2020).

**Link.** <https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html>

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Formalises the four principles of the Data Mesh: domain-oriented decentralised data ownership and architecture, data as a product, self-serve data infrastructure as a platform, and federated computational governance. Keeps operational and analytical data as separate planes and describes a governance that seeks interoperability rather than a single canonical model.

**Family.** Data architecture and management: the mesh model, ownership and stewardship.

## How the playbook uses it

- [[sec-1-underestimated-pillar]]: technology advances that address volume but not the other dimensions of scale, behind the tool-first programme; governance that embraces several contexts rather than one canonical model
- [[sec-2-principle-zero]]: operational and analytical data as two planes, integrated yet separate, a precedent for separating management and consumption
- [[sec-3-lineage]]: the four principles of the Data Mesh, transposed in section 5
- [[sec-5-knowledge-mesh]]: the four principles, mapped one by one; quality assured by the domains rather than by central certification of golden datasets, basis of the fifth principle; governance that embraces multiple interpretive contexts rather than one canonical model
- [[sec-9-field-guide]]: the self-serve platform as the condition for domain autonomy, basis of the trajectory; success measured by use across the mesh rather than volume governed
