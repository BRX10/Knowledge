# Furnas et al. (1987), The Vocabulary Problem

**Citation.** Furnas, G., Landauer, T., Gomez, L. and Dumais, S., The Vocabulary Problem in Human-System Communication, Communications of the ACM 30(11), 964–971 (1987).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Shows experimentally that two people choose the same word for the same object less than 20% of the time. Concludes that systems relying on a single designer’s vocabulary fail most users, and calls for many access routes to the same item.

**Family.** Language and cognition: grounding as the shared basis of joint action; why filing and finding diverge.
