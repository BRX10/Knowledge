# Clark & Brennan (1991), Grounding in Communication

**Citation.** Clark, H. and Brennan, S., Grounding in Communication, in Resnick, L., Levine, J. and Teasley, S. (eds.), Perspectives on Socially Shared Cognition, American Psychological Association, 127–149 (1991).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Defines grounding as the collective process by which participants in a conversation build common ground, the mutual knowledge and beliefs that joint action requires. Shows that the effort needed depends on the purpose and the medium of communication.

**Family.** Language and cognition: grounding as the shared basis of joint action; why filing and finding diverge.
