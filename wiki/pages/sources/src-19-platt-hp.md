# Platt, attributed (1990s)

**Citation.** Attributed to Platt, L., chief executive of Hewlett-Packard (1990s): “If only HP knew what HP knows, we would be three times more productive.” Widely reported; the title of O’Dell, C. and Grayson, C. J., If Only We Knew What We Know: Identification and Transfer of Internal Best Practices, California Management Review 40(3) (1998), echoes it.

**Summary in the playbook.**

> An attributed quotation, reported in several variants (“productive”, “profitable”), with no primary source located. It has become a slogan of knowledge management; O’Dell and Grayson’s article on the internal transfer of best practices takes up its formula in its title.

**Family.** Management practice: the gap between what a company knows and what it uses.
