# Hansen, Nohria & Tierney (1999), strategies for managing knowledge

**Citation.** Hansen, M., Nohria, N. and Tierney, T., What’s Your Strategy for Managing Knowledge?, Harvard Business Review (1999).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Distinguishes two knowledge management strategies: codification, which stores knowledge in documents for reuse, and personalisation, which connects people who hold it. Argues that firms should pursue one predominantly, in line with their competitive strategy.

**Family.** Sociology and organisation: knowledge as distributed, social and lived in communities; why tools alone did not deliver.
