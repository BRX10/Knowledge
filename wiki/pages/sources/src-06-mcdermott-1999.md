# McDermott (1999), why IT cannot deliver KM

**Citation.** McDermott, R., Why Information Technology Inspired but Cannot Deliver Knowledge Management, California Management Review 41(4) (1999), doi:10.2307/41166012.

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Explains why early knowledge management systems disappointed: information technology can store and move information, but knowledge is tied to people, their thinking and their communities. Recommends building communities and combining human and technical systems rather than relying on repositories alone.

**Family.** Sociology and organisation: knowledge as distributed, social and lived in communities; why tools alone did not deliver.
