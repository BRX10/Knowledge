# Montaigne (1580), Essais I, 26

**Citation.** Montaigne, M. de, Essais, I, 26, “De l’institution des enfants” (1580).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Essay on the education of children, in which Montaigne prefers a well-formed judgement to an accumulation of learning. It is the origin of the phrase “plutôt la tête bien faite que bien pleine”.

**Family.** Philosophy: the limits of what can be written down; the well-made mind over the full one.

## How the playbook uses it

- [[sec-closing]]: the well-made mind, quoted: discernment and order above accumulation
