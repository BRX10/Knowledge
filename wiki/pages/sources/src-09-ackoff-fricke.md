# Ackoff (1989); Frické (2009), the DIKW pyramid and its critique

**Citation.** Ackoff, R., From Data to Wisdom, Journal of Applied Systems Analysis 16 (1989); Frické, M., The Knowledge Pyramid: A Critique of the DIKW Hierarchy, Journal of Information Science 35(2) (2009), doi:10.1177/0165551508094050.

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Ackoff popularised the hierarchy from data to information, knowledge and wisdom. Frické criticises it as unsound, arguing that knowledge is not obtained by simply refining data.

**Family.** Information science: what distinguishes knowledge from data and information.
