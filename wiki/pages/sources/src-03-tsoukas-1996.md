# Tsoukas (1996), the firm as a distributed knowledge system

**Citation.** Tsoukas, H., The Firm as a Distributed Knowledge System: A Constructionist Approach, Strategic Management Journal 17(S2), 11–25 (1996).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Argues that a firm’s knowledge is inherently distributed and cannot be surveyed or held as a whole by any single mind, management included. The managerial task is therefore to connect and coordinate knowledge rather than to centralise it.

**Family.** Sociology and organisation: knowledge as distributed, social and lived in communities; why tools alone did not deliver.
