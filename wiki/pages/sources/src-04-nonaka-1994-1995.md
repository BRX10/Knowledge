# Nonaka (1994); Nonaka & Takeuchi (1995)

**Citation.** Nonaka, I., A Dynamic Theory of Organizational Knowledge Creation, Organization Science 5(1), 14–37 (1994); Nonaka, I. and Takeuchi, H., The Knowledge-Creating Company, Oxford University Press (1995).

**Summary in the playbook.**

> Theorises organisational knowledge creation as a continuous conversion between tacit and explicit knowledge, through socialisation, externalisation, combination and internalisation (SECI). The book illustrates it with Japanese companies and shows the role of middle managers in turning individual insight into organisational knowledge.

**Family.** Sociology and organisation: knowledge as distributed, social and lived in communities; why tools alone did not deliver.
